Design direction: "Ledger" - financial analytics aesthetic.
- Palette: Ink #0B0E14 (bg dark), Paper #F7F5F0 (bg light), Slate #5B6472, Signal Green #1F9D55 (positive/buy), Signal Red #C0392B (negative/sell), Amber #C99A2E (accent/warning)
- Type: "Fraunces" (display, used for big metrics/headlines) + "Inter" (body/UI) + "JetBrains Mono" (numbers, tickers, code/ETL snippets)
- Layout: dense data tables, monospace numbers right-aligned, hairline borders (1px), minimal radius (4-6px) - evokes a terminal/ledger not a marketing site
- Signature element: the "score strip" - a horizontal segmented bar showing F-Score style pass/fail criteria, used on dashboard cards and strategy pages
